Confidentiality Mode

Confidentiality Mode is chosen per chat. A normal chat can be upgraded to confidential, but a confidential chat can never be turned back: history kept under the stronger promise stays under it, and anything already sent to a provider cannot be recalled. These settings apply to every chat you mark confidential from here on.

What Confidentiality Mode covers

Confidentiality Mode is built for your own personal data. Documents identifying other people are protected in exactly the same technical way but are not covered — Overseer cannot identify someone who has no account, so it cannot honour an access or erasure request from them — and regulated data such as health, financial or other special-category records is not supported at all, because Overseer holds no BAA or equivalent agreement with any AI provider.

Outbound Masking

Masking is not part of Confidentiality Mode and is not chosen per chat. A class you switch on here is masked in every outbound turn, in every chat, confidential or not, from now on.

What it does: when Overseer recognises a value of a class you have switched on, it replaces that value with a placeholder before the prompt leaves the server, and puts your own value back in the reply. You keep seeing your value; the provider receives the placeholder.

What it does not do: masking reduces accidental credential leakage, it does not guarantee it. A secret with no recognisable shape passes straight through, unmasked.
