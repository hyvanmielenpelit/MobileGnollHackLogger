Multi-Run Analysis

Analysis group
A set of runs of one suite and one model that the statistics are computed over: the pooled Intelligence Index with its uncertainty, speed and cost.
Tier A — Replicate
The runs repeat one condition exactly, so they are pooled into one index with its interval.
Tier B — Quality-comparable
The quality scores are comparable, but timing or pricing differs, so speed and cost are flagged as degraded.
Tier C — Cross-condition
The runs span two conditions, so they are compared with each other and never averaged into one index.

A completed series or battery creates and analyzes its groups automatically.
